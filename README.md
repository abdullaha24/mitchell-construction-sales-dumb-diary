# Sales Diary

A stupidly simple phone app for our field sales team. Each salesman opens his own link and sees:

- **Today's Chase**: who he promised to call or visit today, with overdue ones first
- **Hot Pipeline**: his samples and quotes that are close to becoming orders
- **All My Parties**: every contractor, site and dealer he deals with, searchable

He logs visits, quotes, wins and losses in a couple of taps. Everything lands in a Google Sheet,
and the owner gets his own screen: pipeline by salesman and region, who worked today, what's
overdue or going cold, and why deals were lost.

**Status:** phases 1–2 are live: the Sales Diary sheet, its Check setup menu, and private links (the owner makes them in Sales Diary → Links; a link says "Hello, RIA"). Salesmen can't log anything yet; phase 3 (my sites + add site) is next.
